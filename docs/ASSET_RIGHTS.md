# Asset and data rights

The repository and public demo contain no film footage, music, third-party
logos, or found datasets. All metric sequences, lineage IDs, transforms, test
patterns, and evaluation results are synthetic and authored for Safe Frame.

The three sample clips under `safe_frame/web/samples/` are the only media files
here. They are generated from arithmetic by `scripts/make_samples.py`, contain
no recorded imagery, and can be regenerated byte for byte. The page decodes and
measures them but never plays them, and the two containing a flash burst are
deliberately not offered for download: distributing a file that could trigger a
seizure is the harm this product exists to prevent, so the rule applies to us
as well as to the catalogues we check.

Technology and product names appear only for factual attribution. Safe Frame is
Apache-2.0; dependencies retain their own licenses.
